What this is

A one-to-one port of the cookie clicker hidden inside Harder Hat on Minr. The original is written in MSC and runs in five identical rooms in the Theta world; every price table, drop chance, timing and chat message here comes straight from those scripts. Progress saves in your browser.

How to play

  • Click the cookie. A click only lands if the cooldown has passed since your last landed click, otherwise it flashes red.
  • Every landed click rolls for a golden cookie (worth 20× a click) which appears in one of fourteen spots around the cookie. If the spot it picks is taken, it does not spawn.
  • Each golden spawn has a chance to be a diamond cookie instead, worth 200× a click.
  • Press the stone buttons on the coloured blocks to buy upgrades. The wall lights up a line of particles when something becomes affordable.
  • Milk costs 15,000 to unlock. Click the bucket to multiply every cookie source for a few seconds; it recharges over 45 seconds.
  • Buy the 100,000,000-cookie button to beat the secret.

Notes

The optimiser that ships with the scripts puts the fastest route to 100,000,000 at about 23 minutes of continuous clicking. The sounds are the same ones the room plays, picked and pitched the way the game does it. The reset button on the far left asks you to type “yes” in chat, like the one in the room.
